# Wallpaper Provenance

The seven NewBe wallpaper masters were created on August 19, 2026 with OpenAI's built-in image-generation workflow. No external images were used as generation inputs or style references.

All prompts shared this production specification:

> Official NewBe desktop wallpaper master; polished high-end digital environmental artwork; calm retro-futurist 1990s optimism evolved into a sophisticated 2026 workstation aesthetic; wide cinematic composition; restrained NewBe warm gold, satin aluminum, graphite, warm off-white, and complementary natural tones; original artwork; no people, animals, recognizable brands, logos, text, watermarks, interface elements, excessive neon, bloom, or clutter.

Individual concepts:

- **Desert Dawn:** Serene dunes at first light with a distant modernist observatory, pale stone, satin metal, and a warm-gold vertical accent.
- **Glass Horizon:** A tranquil reflective sea, pale blue sky, translucent architectural planes, curved brushed-metal forms, and subtle warm-gold accents.
- **Brushed Arc:** A monumental brushed-aluminum arc rising from a mirror-still pool, with concentric terraces and a gold inset line.
- **Alpine Sun:** A sunrise alpine basin, snow ridges, golden grasses, a clear lake, and a small glass-and-metal shelter.
- **Coastal Pavilion:** A broad glass-and-metal pavilion on a quiet rocky coast with turquoise water and restrained gold trim.
- **Floating Panels:** Translucent glass, brushed metal, and gold panels floating above a mirror-smooth neutral surface.
- **Moon Reflection:** A moonlit coastal basin, calm water, geometric terraces, and subdued gold edge lighting for dark-mode use.

The PNG files ending in `-source.png` are the unmodified generated masters. The JPEG variants are center-cropped, Lanczos-resampled exports with restrained output sharpening, 4:2:0 chroma subsampling, and quality level 91. Every final asset is tracked in `SHA256SUMS`.
